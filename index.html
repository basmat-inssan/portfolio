Who We Are

A youth-led association with a human mark

Basmat Inssan (بصمة إنسان — "a human fingerprint") is an independent, non-profit, youth-led association based in Morocco. Our name reflects our belief that every act of kindness leaves a lasting mark on a human life.

We exist to stand beside people in need — with dignity, warmth and solidarity — and to inspire a new generation to serve its community with love and responsibility.

  • Independent & non-profit by principle
  • Led by young people, for the whole community
  • Humanity first — always with dignity
